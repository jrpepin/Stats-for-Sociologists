Checkpoint 07

Calculate and interpret the mean salary for professors in this dataset.

mean(Salaries$salary)
[1] 113706.5

On average, professors in this sample earn $113,707.

Calculate the median salary for professors in this dataset.

median(Salaries$salary)
[1] 107300

What does the difference between the mean and median suggest about the data?

The mean is greater than the median, which suggests there are some professors who make substantially more (outliers) than typical professors.

What’s the average number of years since receiving a PhD for professors in this sample? What’s the average years of service? (Notice there is one line per function.)

mean(Salaries$yrs.since.phd)
[1] 22.31486
mean(Salaries$yrs.service)
[1] 17.61461

The average years since PhD is 22 years, whereas the average years of service is 18 years. This suggests a gap between graduating and the time of first position as a professor.
