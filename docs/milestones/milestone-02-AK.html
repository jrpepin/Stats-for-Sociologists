Is the median value for hrs1 consistent with the mean? Speculate what might explain any differences.

The median (40) and mean (39.44) are close, suggesting little skew. The slightly lower mean than median suggests a mild left (e.g., negative) skew, indicating some respondents report very low hours (likely due to unemployment or few part-time hours).

Interpret the mean and standard deviation for hrs1.

On average, Americans work slightly less than a standard full-time schedule of 40 hours per week (39 hours). The standard deviation of 14 hours indicates 68% of respondents report between 25.6 and and 53 weekly work hours. This is a substantial amount of variation, as some people work significantly fewer or more hours per week. This likely reflects a mix of part-time, full-time, and overtime work patterns.
